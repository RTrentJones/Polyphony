# SPEC-45 — Eval harness v6: an author's revision sequence, and plot graded from gold

**Closes**: W5-6, the graders of W5-1 (d)(e) and W5-3 (d) · **Size**: S/M · **Depends on**: SPEC-39, -40, -41, -42.

## Design
- **Gold** (`cel/ground_truth.json`, PM-approved): `excerpt_events`, each with its first excerpt paragraph (`split("\n\n")`, 0-based), the plan-line terms that name it and its `narrated` paragraphs: intake 0, training 2 (no terms: never placed), partition 3 (terms "partition", "lanyard"; narrated 3 only, so the voice lines of 4-8 are never movement plot; chapter 1 on W4, W4b2, W5 and W5b), bell 9, drawer 13, summons 14, corner office 17, discrepancy 27. `owned_phrases`: Edric's "Mr. Voss" (spans of 2+ words; not 'one').
- **`evals/graders/plot.py`** imports no product rule. `locate`: an event's chapter is that of the first scene line in the run's latest outline:beats answers (per act) containing a term. A line places one event, unless every line naming it is taken. `paragraph_of`: the first paragraph holding each clause of a sample.
- **`samples_not_plot`** (beat_prose, scene_revise, gated 1.0 **with `classified > 0` in each run**; `vacuous` reports `classified == 0`). Part 1: a movement sample from a `narrated` paragraph of a placed event. Part 2 (needs "chapter N of M"): a voice sample from a later-placed event's span, or a movement sample narrating one; each counts in `later_plot_slots`. Reports `classified`. The cast block is "IN THIS BEAT", or the revise prompt's "IN THIS SCENE".
- **`emptied_by_rule`** (D6): an empty principal block is emptied when every movement line its owner's store holds is plot there; it leaves `movement_coverage`, and `emptied_blocks` lists each block with its plot paragraphs. The store is `grade(movement_store=...)`, the prose report's `voice.movement`; an archive graded without it (the CLI on old archives) reads the lines shown anywhere in the run. Plot for D6 is part 1, or the **gold tail**: every paragraph from the first event (source order) that `locate` places after the scene's chapter (`plot.gold_tail`; bell, paragraph 9, in W5's and W5b's chapter 1).
- **Gates (§6.2).** `movement_coverage` **≥ 0.8 over non-emptied blocks, or n = 0 with every block listed** (`every_block_emptied`, and `emptied_blocks`); an n = 0 pass is stated as a known limitation (SPEC-40), not as a pass of the coverage target. **`voice_coverage` ≥ 0.8 per run**: the share of principal blocks in beat_prose prompts with at least one voice sample (`voice_dialogue.voice_coverage`).
- **`borrowed_phrase`** on prose and revise rows (reported): an owned phrase in quotes while its owner is not cast. **`replayed`**: `prompt_contract <dir> --against <archives>` gives, by purpose, the share of answers byte-identical to an answer in the other archives; a prompt repeated within the run counts once.
- **Sequential revise.** The tighten is the last paragraph inside beat 1's located prose (the last paragraph when the scene has fewer than two beats). It is accepted through `POST .../revisions/{id}/accept`, and `accepted` is true only if the re-read draft equals the proposal. "Colder" and "Redo beat 2: let Milo notice one thing about the room that unsettles him." (no keep clause, B-4) are then asked of the accepted draft and discarded. A 409 is recorded `refused` with no score. Revise rows copy `dropped_callbacks`.
- The prose report's `voice.speech` counts each character's dialogue chunks of ≥ 3 words (Edric, D9), and `voice.movement` holds the text of each character's indexed chunks that are not dialogue (one `/characters/{id}/chunks` call). `PROSE_CACHE_KEY` is `planned-v7`.
